Welcome

Hello, Coriolis.

Coriolis is a wall clock first: calm, legible from across the room, and on all day without demanding attention. Ask more of it and it turns into a yoga instructor, a workout timer, a focus timer, a fireplace or an arcade.

Chapter 1

Safety and care

Mains voltage inside. The power supply inside the frame is connected to 230 V. Never open the back, touch the wiring or work on the electronics while the power cord is plugged in. Have any mains wiring checked by a qualified person.
  • Use only the supplied mains lead and the fused, switched inlet on the back. Replace the fuse only with the same type (T3.15 A slow-blow).
  • Keep the ventilation slots at the top and bottom of the frame clear. Do not cover the display or hang it directly above a radiator or fireplace.
  • For indoor use in dry rooms only. Keep away from water, steam and direct sunlight.
  • The display weighs about 6 kg. Hang it on a wall cleat rated for at least 15 kg, screwed into a stud or with suitable wall plugs.
  • LED panels can be very bright. Avoid staring into the display at full brightness at close range; Night mode dims it automatically.
  • Keep the remote's coin battery away from children. If swallowed, seek medical help immediately.

Cleaning

Switch off at the inlet. Wipe the front with a dry or barely damp microfibre cloth. Do not use solvents, glass cleaner or abrasive pads on the acrylic front.

Disposal

Electronic equipment and batteries must not go in household waste. Take them to an electronics collection point (in Spain, a punto limpio).

Chapter 2

Getting started

  1. Hang it up. Mount the display on its wall cleat with the power inlet at the bottom. Leave a few centimetres free above and below for airflow.
  2. Plug in and switch on with the switch next to the power inlet on the back.
  3. Watch it wake up. A swirl of light spirals into the Coriolis logo, then the clock appears. Press any button to skip the intro.
  4. Check the time. The internal clock keeps time with its own battery, even when unplugged. If the time is wrong, hold MENU on the remote to open Settings and adjust it under Clock (page 16).
The intro — about two seconds
The clock — the home screen
Coriolis switches between winter and summer time by itself on the last Sundays of March and October (European rule). You never have to change the clock for daylight saving.

Chapter 3

The remote control

Coriolis works with the slim aluminium Apple Remote. Point it at the display; the receiver sits behind the frame's edge.

Arrows

Left / Right — previous / next scene.
Up / Down — change the colour theme.
Inside menus, games and workouts the arrows move, steer and adjust instead.

OK

Start, confirm, pause.

MENU

Tap — open the scene menu (tap again to close).
Hold 1 s — open Settings.

Play/Pause

Tap — same as OK (pause a workout from the mat).
Hold 1 s — go home to the clock.

Holding a button

In menus, holding an arrow scrolls quickly. In games, holding an arrow keeps steering (for example, keeps the Pong paddle moving).

Back

The Apple Remote has no separate Back button. Use MENU to close menus, and hold Play/Pause to leave whatever you are doing and return to the clock.

Another remote not working? Its button codes can be added to the firmware. The display reports every code it receives over its USB port (see the Build Guide).

Chapter 4

Everyday use

Coriolis shows one scene at a time: a clock, an animation, a game or a guided session. The clock is home; you can always get back to it by holding Play/Pause.

Clock — time, date and a seconds bar
Clock overlay on top of an animation

Browse scenes

Press Right or Left to slide to the next or previous scene. Its name appears briefly at the bottom of the display.

Colour themes

Press Up or Down on a clock or animation to change its colour theme: Rainbow, Heat, Ocean, Forest, Ice, Gray and Matrix. Guides and games keep their own colours so they stay easy to read.

Clock overlay

Keep the time visible over any animation: choose digital, analog or word style, position and size in Settings → Overlay, or press 0 on a 17-button remote.

Autoplay

Turn on Settings → General → Auto to cycle through clocks and animations automatically (every 30 seconds by default). Games and workouts are never interrupted.

Yoga

Poses are held for 15 seconds by default (5 – 60 s). One-sided poses are done on the right and then the left: a tag shows the side, the figure mirrors itself and the coach says “switch sides”. For the last seconds of each pose a Next badge shows what comes next, with soft ticks; a chime marks the halfway point of longer holds.

Guided fitness12

Breathe and Focus

Breathe

Breathe in as the circle grows, hold while it stays, breathe out as it shrinks. Box breathing uses four equal phases; 4-7-8 has a long hold and a longer exhale to help you wind down. Up/Down sets the pace (3 – 8 s per in-breath); a soft tone marks each phase.

Focus

A Pomodoro timer for the wall. Work for 25 minutes (Up/Down: 5 – 60), then take a 5-minute break; every fourth break is 15 minutes long. The ring drains as time runs, and a dot appears for each finished session. OK pauses.

Chapter 11

Sound

The built-in speaker plays chimes, countdown ticks, the spoken coach (pose names, “switch sides”, rep counts), the fireplace crackle and game sounds. Everything plays from a memory card inside the audio module; Coriolis never connects to the internet.

Volume

Settings → General → Volume, from 0 (mute) to 30.

The sound card

The audio module has its own microSD card (FAT32, up to 32 GB) with three folders at the top level:

01Chimes, bells, ticks and game sounds
02Spoken guidance: pose and move names, numbers, prompts
ADVERTCopies of the chimes, played over the fireplace crackle

To create or replace it, run python tools/generate_voice_pack.py on a computer and copy the three folders to the card's root. You can choose another voice with --voice. Do not rename the numbered files.

Troubleshooting

ProblemTry this
Nothing on the displayCheck the mains lead and the switch on the back. Check the fuse in the inlet.
Very dim displayIt may be night-time: see Settings → Night. Otherwise raise Settings → General → Bright.
Time is wrongSettings → Clock: check Zone (UTC+1 for Spain) and Summer (EU auto), then the hour.
Time resets after unpluggingReplace the clock battery (CR2032) on the controller board.
Remote doesn't respondReplace its coin battery and point it at the frame edge with the receiver. Bright sunlight on the receiver can blind it.
Arrows don't change sceneYou are in a game or workout, which uses the arrows. Tap MENU, or hold Play/Pause to go home.
Stuck in a menuTap MENU to close it; hold Play/Pause to go home.
No soundCheck Volume in Settings; check the sound card is inserted and has the 01, 02 and ADVERT folders.
Picture upside down or sidewaysSettings → General → Rotate.
Weather says it's waitingThe optional weather module isn't fitted or has no connection yet.
A bright pixel or dark lineSwitch off for 10 seconds and on again. If it stays, a panel or its cable needs attention (see the Build Guide).

Specifications

Display128 × 128 RGB LEDs (16,384 pixels), four 64 × 64 panels, 24-bit colour, ~120 Hz refresh
SizeImage 512 × 512 mm with 4 mm pixels (P4), or 384 × 384 mm with 3 mm pixels (P3). Frame about 550 × 550 × 80 mm (P4)
WeightAbout 5–6 kg (P4)
ControllerTeensy 4.1 (600 MHz ARM Cortex-M7) with SmartLED Shield V5
ClockBattery-backed real-time clock (CR2032); automatic EU summer time
ControlInfrared remote (Apple Remote or 17-button NEC remote); USB for servicing
SoundDFPlayer Mini, 3 W speaker, offline clips from microSD
Power230 V AC 50 Hz in; internal 5 V 40 A supply. Typical 10–30 W showing the clock; up to ~100 W on full white
NetworkNone. Coriolis works fully offline.
Content25 scenes: 6 clocks/info · 4 fitness coaches (5 yoga flows, 4 exercise programs) · 4 games · 11 ambient animations
